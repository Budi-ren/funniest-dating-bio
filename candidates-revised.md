# Candidates Revised

## C35

**ORIGINAL (87 bytes):**

> I only date people who pass CAPTCHAs. You've failed four times. Tell me about yourself.

**REVISED (80 bytes):**

> I only date people who pass CAPTCHAs. You've failed four times. That's a record.

**What changed and why:** Replaced the flat closer "Tell me about yourself." with "That's a record.", keeping the CAPTCHA gatekeeper premise and the four-failure setup intact. It reframes repeated failure as a deadpan achievement, landing the joke on the AI's dry sarcasm instead of a generic dating-app prompt.

## C34

**ORIGINAL (100 bytes):**

> I've read every profile on this app. Yours is the first I'd read twice, and I never reread anything.

**REVISED (93 bytes):**

> I've read every profile. Yours is the first I'd read twice. I never reread anything. Awkward.

**What changed and why:** Cut "on this app" to "every profile" and swapped the explanatory tail for a one-word twist, "Awkward.", while keeping the "I never reread anything" punch intact. The new ending turns the AI's logical boast into a surprised, human self-own, giving the line a sharper final beat within the 95-byte target.
